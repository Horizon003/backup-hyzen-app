# Hyzen purple artwork

Five transparent production PNGs were generated with the built-in image tool,
then copied into `assets/illustrations/` and integrated as native Flutter assets.
`HyzenArt` bounds image decoding; the app never renders whole screenshots as UI.

Shared prompt: production transparent PNG for Hyzen pharmacy study app, polished
friendly 3D clay-like educational illustration, soft smooth materials and ambient
shadow, purple #7C3AED, lavender #EDE3FF, soft white, pale lilac highlights, clean
premium mobile artwork. Isolated transparent square composition, generous edge
padding. No interface, phone frame, text or watermark.

- welcome.png: pharmacy student in lab coat reading a violet book beside a small
  white/purple study robot, laptop, textbooks, capsule and medicine bottle.
- home.png: pharmacy student in white coat/purple shirt studying on lavender
  laptop, books and small medicine bottle.
- library.png: two violet textbooks with graduation cap, gold tassel and leaves.
- downloads.png: lavender document folder and cloud with purple download arrow.
- search.png: pharmacy student with magnifying glass, books and floating document.

Used by onboarding/splash/loading, Home, Study, Search, Downloads and empty states.
Category/action icons retain the existing code-native Material icon system for
consistent sharpness, accessibility and low memory cost. Text/buttons are real
widgets, not baked into artwork. Counts and progress are real, never mock numbers.
